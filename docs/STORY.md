# Story map: Connecting places. Explaining decisions.

An original, source-linked narrative by Jacob Analem, reviewed 23 November 2026. Access the Story map navigation tab, or append `#story-map` to the website address.

## Structure
1. Canadian context: introduce how geography connects infrastructure decisions.
2. Low Level Road: transport and community trade-offs; approximate North Vancouver reference.
3. Brucejack transmission assessment: terrain, GIS, LiDAR and field verification; approximate regional reference, not the actual transmission route.
4. Atlin Hydro Expansion: seasonal water availability and a First Nation-owned energy project; approximate town reference, not the storage facility footprint.
5. Waterdown to Finch: traceable survey/utility records and asset data; regional corridor reference, not a pipeline alignment.
6. Independent GIS demonstration: transition to the hypothetical Howe Sound analysis already present in the portfolio.

## Interaction
Scroll chapters or use numbered chapter buttons and previous/next controls. The map pans to a chapter's geographic context. Marker clicks select chapters. Source links open official pages. The final chapter opens the existing route analysis or methods view. The link-copy control copies a direct link to the story. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. The map remains visible above narrative on mobile.
